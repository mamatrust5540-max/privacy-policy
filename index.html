Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the QR & Barcode Scanner mobile application ("App") provided by us. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how our application operates.

Privacy Commitment: We respect your data. We do NOT collect, sell, or transmit your scanned codes, personal files, notes, or contacts to external servers. All scanning, generation, and processing happens directly on your device.

1. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Required solely to detect and scan QR codes and barcodes in real-time. Camera frames are processed on-device via Google ML Kit / barcode APIs and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • Photos & Media Access: Used exclusively when you choose to scan a barcode from an existing image in your gallery or save generated QR codes/PDFs to your device.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Provides optional haptic feedback when a scan is successfully detected.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to load web previews, open URLs upon your action, and serve advertisements/app updates.
  • In-App Purchases / Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): Used if you choose to purchase premium features or remove ads. All payment transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play Billing.

2. On-Device Processing & Data Storage

Your scan history, custom generated codes, and favorites are stored locally in an encrypted/private SQLite database on your device. You can clear or export your history at any time from the app settings. We do not maintain copies on any remote servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the app free, we may display advertisements via Google AdMob and use Google Play Services. These third-party providers may collect anonymized device identifiers and performance telemetry in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Policies

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete your scan history at any time from inside the app, or delete all app data by clearing the app cache or uninstalling the app.
